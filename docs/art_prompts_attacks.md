# Prompts für die Angriffe im Stehen

Anhänge jeweils: 1. das Master-Bild des Kämpfers, 2. die genannte Posen-Vorlage (`assets/art/guides/`, Dateiname klein geschrieben).

## Konrad – lp (3 Frames) – Vorlage `konrad_lp_guide.png`
```
Edit the first attached image (the master sprite of Konrad): keep the character exactly as he is - the same face, hair, body, costume, colours, pixel-art style and the same scale. Draw him in the 3 poses of the second attached image, a pose guide with 3 mannequins, one per frame, from left to right.
In the mannequins, the ORANGE arm and leg are his LEAD arm and leg (the front ones, towards the opponent on the right), the BLUE arm and leg are his REAR arm and leg. Match the positions of hands, feet, knees, elbows, hips and the lean of the torso as closely as possible; the mannequin is only a stick figure, so give him his real body, costume and muscles.
Animation: light punch (jab) with the LEAD fist. 1: the lead fist starts forward. 2: lead arm fully extended at head height, fist clenched. 3: the arm comes back halfway to the guard. The rear fist stays in the guard at the chin.
Output: one sprite sheet, a single row of 3 cells of exactly 256 x 256 px (image 768 x 256 px), solid flat magenta background #FF00FF, no shadow, no text, no frame numbers, no magenta, pink or purple on the character. Facing right. Feet on the floor at y = 239 (the black line in the guide). Same size as the master in every frame - never enlarge or shrink him.
```
