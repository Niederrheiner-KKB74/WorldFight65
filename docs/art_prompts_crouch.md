# Prompts für die Hock-Angriffe

Anhänge jeweils: 1. Master-Bild, 2. Hocke (`crouch`) des Kämpfers, 3. die Posen-Vorlage aus `assets/art/guides/`.

## Konrad – c_lp (3 Frames) – Vorlage `konrad_c_lp_guide.png`
```
Edit the first attached image (the master sprite of Konrad): keep the character exactly as he is - the same face, hair, body, costume, colours, pixel-art style and the same scale. The second attached image shows him crouching: all frames of this animation start from this crouch and have its size (about two thirds of his standing height). Draw him in the 3 poses of the third attached image, a pose guide with 3 mannequins, one per frame, from left to right.
In the mannequins, the ORANGE arm and leg are his LEAD arm and leg (the front ones, towards the opponent on the right), the BLUE arm and leg are his REAR arm and leg. Match the positions of hands, feet, knees, hips and the lean of the torso as closely as possible; the mannequin is only a stick figure, so give him his real body, costume and muscles. Dashed grey arrows only show a movement - do not draw them.
Animation: crouching punch, 3 frames. 1: from the crouch, the lead fist starts forward. 2: lead arm fully extended forward at the height of a standing opponent's stomach, body leaning into it. 3: the arm comes back towards the guard.
Output: one sprite sheet, a single row of 3 cells of exactly 256 x 256 px (image 768 x 256 px), solid flat magenta background #FF00FF, no shadow, no text, no frame numbers, no magenta, pink or purple on the character. Facing right. Feet on the floor at y = 239 (the black line in the guide). He stays crouched and as small as in the crouch image in every frame - never enlarge him to fill the cell.
```
